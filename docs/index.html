How it works

  1. You describe the idea. Qwen turns the sentence into a setup: stock, direction, size, leverage, entry time, and the conditions you named. You can edit every part.
  2. Precedent retrieves the precedents. It searches every closed-market window on record for this token on Bitget and keeps the ones that match your conditions at your entry time.
  3. It shows the distribution. How each one ended after a 0.30% round trip in fees and slippage, the ride on the way, and how the same trade did with no conditions at all.
  4. It runs the stress tests. Worst night on record, doubled volatility, liquidation distance, the live Bitget order book for your size, and whether the pattern still held in the recent half.
  5. You decide. Precedent never places an order. Save the idea to the notebook and it will show you what happened.

History is short and is not a forecast. Shorts and leverage assume the matching Bitget stock futures; prices here are spot rTokens. Not financial advice.
