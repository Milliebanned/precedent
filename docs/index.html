How it works

From a sentence to a decision, in about fifteen seconds.

  1. 01

    You describe the idea

    In plain English. Qwen turns the sentence into a setup: token, direction, size, leverage, entry time and the conditions you named. Every part stays editable.

  2. 02

    It retrieves the precedents

    Every closed-market night and weekend on record for that token on Bitget is searched. The ones that match your conditions at your entry time are kept.

  3. 03

    It shows how they ended

    Each precedent after a 0.30% round trip in fees and slippage, the ride on the way there, and the same trade with no conditions as a baseline.

  4. 04

    It tries to break the idea

    Nine stress tests, from the worst night on record to the live Bitget order book for your size, each checked against your own house rules.

  5. 05

    You decide

    A short memo sums up the numbers. Precedent never places an order. Save the idea and the notebook shows what happened once the window closed.

Stress tests

Nine ways an idea can fail, checked before it costs you anything.

  • Worst precedentThe most a matching night has lost, against the most you allow.
  • Worst night on recordThe same trade on the single worst window, conditions or not.
  • Volatility doublesEvery precedent replayed with moves twice as large.
  • Liquidation distanceHow far away it is in normal days, and whether the ride ever got there.
  • Stop-outsHow often your stop would have been hit before the open.
  • Cost shareHow much of a typical move the fees and slippage eat.
  • Recent halfWhether the pattern still paid in the latest precedents, or only early on.
  • Condition checkWhether your condition beats the same trade with no conditions, and on the other nine tokens.
  • Live order bookWhat your size costs to fill on Bitget right now, against the cost assumed.

Why it exists

The idea that started this looked brilliant. Then it met a month it had never seen.

+2.19%Sharpe 5.56 on the 45 sessions it was chosen from
−0.04%Sharpe −0.21 on the next month, unseen

We tested 144 versions of one story: follow Bitcoin’s overnight move into the stock open. The best one, trading the Coinbase token two hours before the bell, was superb on the data it was picked from and flat on the month after.

Most trade ideas are never checked at all, because checking takes an evening and the open is in two hours. Precedent does the check in seconds, and always asks the question that caught us out: does it still work?

The study’s code and results are in the repository.
